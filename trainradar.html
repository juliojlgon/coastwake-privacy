trainRadar privacy policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026. Publisher: Julio. Privacy and support contact: juliojdeveloper@gmail.com.

trainRadar is an independent Netherlands rail information app. It has no user accounts, advertising, publisher analytics, payments or publisher-operated data storage service.

Your location

When you choose Find my location, Android asks for permission to access your approximate or precise location while using the app. The location is used to centre the map and display your position, or to choose your nearest Netherlands station as the journey origin. A single foreground location request stops after a result, a timeout, or when the screen leaves the foreground. Your latest fix remains in app memory while the process is running; it is not saved as a GPS history. The selected origin station is saved with your journey preferences. It is not used for advertising, background tracking or sent as a location parameter to the train API. You may decline permission and continue browsing and planning manually. You can revoke permission in Android Settings. Map requests after centring on your position can reveal the surrounding area to the map provider.

Rail information and maps

The app directly requests rail data from TrackViewer at trackviewer.app. Searches transmit the chosen station names/codes, journey time and language; following a train transmits its service number. Like other internet services, the provider receives network information such as your IP address and request metadata. These requests are necessary for the requested rail functionality. TrackViewer identifies NS and NDOV as its upstream sources. The publisher does not control TrackViewer's server retention. See https://trackviewer.app/handleiding.html for its service information and contact.

MapLibre renders maps from OpenFreeMap. Tile, style and font requests disclose your displayed map area, IP address and ordinary connection metadata to OpenFreeMap and its infrastructure providers. Its current privacy policy describes error logs that may contain IP addresses and requested URLs, deleted after seven days, and temporary security logging for up to thirty days. See https://openfreemap.org/privacy/ for current details.

Optional English notice translation

Google ML Kit translates Dutch disruption text on your device; the source text and translation results are not sent to Google for translation. A language model is downloaded over Wi-Fi. ML Kit also contacts Google for model/configuration updates and sends usage and diagnostic metrics. Google documents device information, app/package information, installation identifiers, feature events, performance and error information, and configured source/destination languages. Google processes these under its privacy policy. See https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms, https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure and https://policies.google.com/privacy.

Storage, security and deletion

Your selected journey, recent origin/destination, language and map preferences are stored in private app storage. Translated notices are cached locally (up to 96 entries); downloaded language models also occupy device storage. Map resources are also cached locally; connection recovery or a manual map refresh may clear these cached resources and download them again. The app disables Android backup and uses HTTPS for its service requests. No system is completely secure.

Use Android Settings → Apps → trainRadar → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app, to remove app-managed local data. Android/Google Play services may manage their own downloaded model files and diagnostic information under their own settings and retention policies. The publisher does not receive a copy of your journeys or GPS history and cannot delete third-party server logs. Contact the relevant provider for requests about their processing, or email the publisher for help.
